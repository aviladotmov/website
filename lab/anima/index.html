Welcome

From storyboard to animatic, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: your files stay on your computer.

Work in progress. This app may have bugs. Save often and keep a copy of important projects (Export › Project with media).

About your files: the project saves your scenes, notes and anything you make here (drawings, pasted images and GIFs, voice-overs). Imported videos and images are not copied. They stay where they are on your computer, and the project only saves their names. If you move or rename them, you will need to relink them.
